Maps & data

Shapefiles and base maps, without the hunt.

Half the work in mapping is just finding the data. I track down the shapefiles a project needs and build clean base maps a company can actually work from. If you have spent a week hunting down layers and building your base maps, there is a good chance I can do it in a day.

County Parcel Shapefiles

Parcel boundaries and ownership from county assessor and GIS offices, pulled for the county you are working.

Texas Railroad Commission Data

Surveys and abstracts, well locations, units, and pipelines from the Railroad Commission's GIS data.

Oklahoma STR & PLSS Grids

Section, township, and range grids for Oklahoma, ready to drop straight into your map.

Well Locations

Surface and bottomhole well points from the state regulators across Oklahoma, Texas, and the surrounding states.

Federal, Tribal & Surface Management

BLM surface management, federal and tribal lands, and surface ownership layers where they are published.

Base Layers

Roads, hydrology, county lines, aerial imagery, floodplain, and soils to round out a working map.

Before you spend too much time searching for shapefiles, ask me first.

There is a good chance I already know where it lives, or can have it to you faster than a data vendor. I also build custom base maps for companies: project and area-of-interest maps, ownership plats, and lease maps, set up the way your team likes to work.

About

Meet the landman behind the work.

Michael McCracken has worked in the land business since 2004. He started McCracken Land Services so brokers and clients could work directly with an experienced landman instead of being handed down the line to whoever happens to be free.

His experience covers title examination and curative, mineral and surface ownership research, leasing and right of way, due diligence, courthouse and Corporation Commission research, and GIS mapping and deed plotting. If it touches land, title, or minerals, he has done it.

The approach is simple. Do the work right, and get it back when you said you would. Brokers and clients keep coming back because the details get handled and nothing slips through.
