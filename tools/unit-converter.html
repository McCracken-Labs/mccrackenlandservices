Land Measurement Converter

Type a value, pick the unit, and see it in feet and every other surveying unit. Varas, rods, chains, links, yards, furlongs, miles, and meters. It runs in your browser, so nothing is uploaded.

Tip: the Texas vara is 33⅓ inches, so 36 varas equals 100 feet. A chain is 66 feet, which is why 10 square chains make an acre.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded or stored.
