How it works

You don't have to do anything.

We set the whole thing up when we build your site. From then on it just shows up.

1

We switch it on

When McCracken Labs builds your site, we turn Scissortail on for you. There is nothing for you to sign up for or configure.

2

It counts, quietly

It tallies visits in the background with no cookies and no personal data. Your visitors never see a banner and your pages stay fast.

3

You get the email

A clean report arrives every week, or every month if you would rather. Want to change how often? Just reply to it.

Built to not know things about people.

  • No cookies, so there is no cookie banner to bolt onto your site.
  • No IP addresses stored. The address is coarsened and hashed with a daily secret, then thrown away.
  • No profile that follows a person from one site to the next.
  • Honors Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control automatically.
  • Counts how many separate people came without keeping anything that names them.
  • Your numbers stay in a database we run for you, not sold off to some ad network.
  • Small and fast, so it will not slow your pages down.
  • The reports are yours. We never share your traffic with anyone.

What you learn

The things worth knowing, and nothing you have to hide.

How many people came

Page views and separate visitors, with an arrow showing whether you are up or down from the last stretch.

What they read

Your most-viewed pages, ranked, so you can see what is pulling people in and do more of it.

Where they found you

Whether visitors came from Google, Facebook, a link somewhere, or typed you in directly.

Roughly where they are

The general areas your visitors are in, handy for a local shop that wants to know it is reaching its town.
